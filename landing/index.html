How to play

Three steps to wizardry

Blocks carrying words fall toward you. Type each word on the keypad before it lands.

STEP 01

Words incoming

Every enemy is a block carrying a word, falling toward the grey block at the bottom of the screen: you. Destroy it before it gets there.

1  2ABC 3DEF 4GHI 5JKL 6MNO 7PQRS 8TUV 9WXYZ

wizard = 9 4 9 2 7 3

STEP 02

Work your T9 magic

Press each letter's number key once, so wizard is 949273. Your first key locks onto a matching word. Finish its code to blast it.

Once you're locked on, wrong keys are simply ignored, so stay calm and keep typing.

  • 3LIVES
  • 5WORLDS
  • 50WAVES
  • 5BOSSES
STEP 03

Survive the onslaught

Let a word reach you and you lose a life. Clear ten waves to face the world's boss, then type whole sentences to chip it down. Words get longer every world.

Flawless words grow your score multiplier. Grab rising powerups for extra lives and more.

Your flip phone is the controller. Your typing is the weapon.

The flip-phone factor

Built for the buttons. Not the touchscreen.

T9 Wizard brings fast-paced typing gameplay to KaiOS flip phones. Instead of tapping a screen or reaching for a full keyboard, you play with the physical keypad already in your hand.

  • 9

    Physical T9 controls

    Every word is typed with the 2–9 keys. The whole game is designed around the keypad.

  • !

    Typing under pressure

    React fast as words descend. The words get longer and the screen gets busier world after world.
